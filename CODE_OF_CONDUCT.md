# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want sllm to be a project anyone can take part in. Everyone involved —
contributors, maintainers, and people opening issues — is expected to help keep
it that way.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful. Disagree with the idea, not the person.
- Assume good faith. Most confusion is a missing explanation, not bad intent.
- Accept feedback gracefully, and give it kindly.
- Keep discussion focused on the work.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory comments about anyone's identity or background.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions,
commit messages — and whenever someone is representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainer via the email on the
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/six5536), or by opening a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/six5536/sllm/security) if you
would rather not use email.

Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer will respond as quickly as
they reasonably can, and may warn, temporarily block, or permanently ban anyone
whose behaviour is judged to breach this policy.
